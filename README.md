# noc-noc

> *Knock knock. Who's there? Your occupation code.*

To apply for Express Entry, a work permit or most provincial programs, newcomers to Canada have to name their
occupation's **NOC code**, one of 516 unit groups in the National Occupational Classification. Picking the wrong
one is a common and costly mistake, and job titles rarely match the official names.

**noc-noc** finds the right NOC 2021 codes for a job title or description, and shows which official duties match.
Every retrieval method is measured on a held-out benchmark built from Statistics Canada's own data.

🚧 Work in progress.

## Data

NOC 2021 Version 1.0 from Statistics Canada, under the
[Statistics Canada Open Licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/terms-conditions/open-licence).

**Not immigration advice.** Always confirm your NOC code on
[canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/find-national-occupation-code.html).
